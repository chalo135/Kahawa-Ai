About Kahawa Smart

Kahawa Smart is an AI-powered coffee leaf disease detection system developed as a final year research project at Multimedia University of Kenya, Faculty of Computing and Information Technology. The system uses a fine-tuned MobileNetV2 deep learning model trained on the JMuBEN coffee leaf dataset collected from Mutira plantation in Kirinyaga County.

Designed specifically for smallholder coffee farmers in East Africa, the system provides instant disease diagnosis through a mobile-friendly web interface in both English and Swahili — without requiring specialized agricultural expertise.

Essential Facts

Core knowledge every coffee farmer should know

Loading live updates...
Second most traded commodity
Coffee is the second most traded commodity in the world after oil, with over 2.25 billion cups consumed every day globally.
Kenya's coffee heritage
Kenya produces some of the world's most prized Arabica coffee. The country exports over 50,000 metric tonnes annually, supporting over 700,000 farming families.
The rust fungus
Coffee leaf rust is caused by Hemileia vastatrix — a fungus that devastated Sri Lanka's entire coffee industry in 1869, turning the country to tea production instead.
Moisture spreads rust
Rust spores need only 24 hours of moisture to germinate. A single rainy season can spread rust across an entire farm, making early detection absolutely critical.
Temperature matters
Leaf rust thrives between 15°C and 28°C — exactly the temperature range of East African highland coffee regions. Climate change is expanding this danger zone upward in altitude.
Over 50 known races
There are more than 50 known races of Hemileia vastatrix. This genetic diversity makes it extremely challenging to develop fully resistant coffee varieties.
AI changes everything
Traditional rust detection requires trained agronomists doing manual inspections. AI systems like Kahawa Smart give any farmer instant diagnosis from a smartphone photo.
Prevention beats cure
Applying copper-based fungicide preventively costs 3–5 times less than treating an active outbreak. Early AI detection makes preventive action possible before visible symptoms appear.
Mobile-first Africa
Over 90% of internet access in rural Kenya is through smartphones. Kahawa Smart is designed mobile-first so any farmer with a phone can use it immediately.
The global coffee belt
Coffee only grows in a narrow band around the equator called the Coffee Belt. Kenya, Ethiopia, Uganda, and Tanzania all sit perfectly within this zone.
5 years to first harvest
A coffee plant takes 3–5 years from planting to produce its first commercial harvest. A rust outbreak can destroy years of investment in under three weeks.
Copper is the shield
Copper-based fungicides like Kocide have been used to control coffee leaf rust since the 1880s. Applied correctly, they are 85–95% effective in preventing new infections.
